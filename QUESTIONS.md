# Foundation Physics Deck

## Subject: Physics
### Topic: Thermodynamics
#### Concept: First Law of Thermodynamics
**Difficulty: Easy**
**Tags: Energy, Laws**

---
Q: Which equation correctly represents the First Law of Thermodynamics?
A) ΔU = Q + W
B) ΔU = Q - W
C) ΔU = Q / W
D) Q = ΔU / W
Correct: B
Explanation: ΔU = Q - W represents the change in internal energy as heat added minus work done by the system.
---

#### Concept: Entropy
**Difficulty: Medium**
**Tags: Second Law, Entropy**

---
Q: What characterizes a reversible process in terms of entropy?
A) Total entropy increases
B) Total entropy decreases
C) Total entropy remains constant
D) System entropy always becomes zero
Correct: C
Explanation: For a reversible process, the total entropy change of the system and surroundings at equilibrium is zero.
